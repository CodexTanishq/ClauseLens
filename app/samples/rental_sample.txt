RESIDENTIAL LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Leave and License Agreement is made and entered into at Mumbai on this 1st day of May 2024, by and between:

LICENSOR:
Apex Horizon Properties Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, having its registered office at Nariman Point, Mumbai 400021, Maharashtra (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensor").

AND

LICENSEE:
Vertex Solutions LLP, having its office at Bandra Kurla Complex, Mumbai 400051, Maharashtra (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensee").

1. LICENSED PREMISES
The Licensor hereby grants leave and license to the Licensee to occupy Apartment 4B, Horizon Towers, Worli, Mumbai, solely for residential purposes.

2. TERM AND DURATION
The duration of this License shall be for a fixed term of eleven (11) months, commencing from 1st May 2024 and expiring on 31st March 2025.

3. LICENSE FEE AND SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Licensee shall pay a monthly license fee of Rs. 65,000 payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar month. The Licensee has deposited an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 1,30,000.

4. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
Upon expiry of the eleven-month term, this agreement shall automatically renew for a further period of eleven (11) months unless either party provides at least 15 days written notice of intention not to renew prior to expiration.

5. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE
Either party may terminate this agreement at any time during the term by giving thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other party without assigning any reason.

6. ASSIGNMENT
The Licensee shall not assign, transfer, sub-license, or share possession of the licensed premises or any portion thereof with any third party.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of England and Wales, and the courts of London shall have exclusive jurisdiction over all disputes arising hereunder.

8. STAMP DUTY AND REGISTRATION
This agreement is executed on appropriate stamp paper, and the parties agree to register this agreement as required under the applicable Maharashtra Stamp Act and registration laws.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Licensor and the Licensee have signed and executed this agreement on the day and year first written above.
