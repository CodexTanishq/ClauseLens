Your annual Cost to Company (CTC) shall be Rs. 18,00,000 payable in monthly installments as per standard payroll procedures.

For a period of twelve (12) months following the end of employment or after termination for any reason, the employee agrees to a non-compete covenant and shall not directly or indirectly join, consult for, or establish any enterprise competing with the Company in India.

In consideration of specialized training provided during the initial term, you agree to an employment bond requiring reimbursement of liquidated damages in the amount of Rs. 2,00,000 if you resign within the first twelve (12) months of employment.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
The total aggregate liability of the Company towards the Employee arising out of or related to this agreement shall be capped at three months basic salary.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, and the competent courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
